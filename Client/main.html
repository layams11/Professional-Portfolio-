01 — Who I Am

A little about
me.

I started when... I was 14, redesigning my school's ugly notice board poster and thinking — I could do this better. I did. And I kept going.

Then I realized... that design without code is just decoration, and code without design is just machinery. I wanted to build things that were both beautiful and functional. So I taught myself to do both.

Now I... build real things. From websites to brand identities to content strategies, I don't wait for permission or a job title to do serious work. I'm a student who ships.

03 — Proof of Work

Case Studies

Project 01
The Brand Refresh
Complete visual identity redesign for a local business — logo, palette, and digital presence.
Design Branding Strategy

The Challenge

A small local business with great products but zero visual consistency — random fonts, clashing colors, no identity. Customers couldn't remember them.

What I Did

Conducted a brand audit, developed 3 identity concepts, presented to the client, and delivered a full brand kit including logo suite, color system, and usage guidelines.

The Outcome

New branding launched across Instagram and packaging. Engagement up 40% in first month. Client described it as "finally feeling like a real brand."

Project 02
The Portfolio Site
Designed and built a personal portfolio from scratch — this one, actually.
Code Design UX

The Challenge

Every student portfolio looks the same. The challenge was building something that felt premium, personal, and memorable — without a template in sight.

What I Did

Wrote a full PRD, designed in Figma, coded entirely from scratch in HTML/CSS/JS with custom animations, scroll reveals, and interactive components.

The Outcome

A portfolio that reflects actual skill and personality. Lighthouse score 94+. Got 3 messages in the first week of sharing it.

Project 03
The Content System
Built a 90-day content strategy and visual system for a student-run Instagram account.
Content Strategy Design

The Challenge

An active account with 800 followers but inconsistent posting, no strategy, and declining engagement. Content felt scattered and purposeless.

What I Did

Ran a content audit, defined 4 content pillars, built a posting calendar, created templates in Canva, and trained the team on the new system.

The Outcome

Engagement rate tripled in 6 weeks. Follower count reached 1.2K. The team now runs the system independently.
